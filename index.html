Summary. No account with email, phone or password is required. We do not collect your real name, address, phone number, contacts, location, photos or microphone. The game is for players aged 16 and over and needs an internet connection. You choose a nickname; it and your score are visible to other players in the ranking. Notifications, statistics and personalised ads are optional and depend on your consent.

Age limit. The game is for players aged 16 and over. We do not knowingly collect personal data from under-16s. If you believe a child's data reached us, write to kodprogaming@gmail.com and we will delete it.

On-device data and backup. Your progress (best score, crystals, purchases, settings, daily reward streak) is stored on your phone. If Android backup is on, it can be backed up to your Google account and restored when you reinstall the game (backup is run by Google; you can switch it off in your phone settings). Uninstalling removes on-device data.

Connection check. To detect whether you are online, the game regularly sends an empty request to a Google server (gstatic.com); Google technically processes your IP address in doing so. Basis: legitimate interest (GDPR Art. 6(1)(f)).

Nickname and ranking. On first launch you choose a nickname (do not use your real name). Your nickname and your best score of the month are stored in Google Firebase (Firestore) and are visible to other players. Sign-in is anonymous: Firebase generates a random user ID. The ranking resets every month; the top 3 players get virtual in-game crystals (no cash prizes). Basis: performance of a contract (Art. 6(1)(b)). Inappropriate names are blocked or may be removed.

Notifications (optional). You turn them on at first launch or in the menu and can turn them off at any time. If on: (1) local reminders generated on your phone (the hour you play most is computed on your phone only and not sent to a server); (2) via Google Firebase Cloud Messaging an identifier is assigned to your device and it subscribes to a language group (e.g. “Turkish”) so we can send announcements. Basis: consent (Art. 6(1)(a)). If you turn it off, the subscription is cancelled.

Statistics and crash reports (optional, OFF by default). If you switch this on on the Legal info screen, Google Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics process a random app-specific identifier, device model, Android version, app version, crash logs and a few events (game start/end, watched ad, purchase, tutorial). Basis: consent (Art. 6(1)(a)).

Ads. The Android app shows Google AdMob ads. In Europe (EU/UK) Google's consent dialog appears at first launch. If you consent, your advertising ID and device information may be used for personalised ads; if you do not, only non-personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice any time under Legal info → Ad preferences. Google may process limited technical data (e.g. IP address, device info) to serve and measure ads. Basis: consent (Art. 6(1)(a)).

Purchases. Payments are processed by Google Play. We never see or store your payment details. For purchase verification we only receive the purchased product ID from Google Play.

App rating. Sometimes Google Play's in-app review dialog is shown. It is run by Google Play and does not tell us whether you rated.

Web version. The web version may be hosted on Firebase Hosting; Google may temporarily process your IP address in server logs. Game data is stored only in your browser.

Version check, settings and announcements. When the game starts and from time to time it reads a public settings document from Google Firebase (Firestore): the minimum required app version, power-up prices and in-game announcements. No personal data or identifier of yours is sent in this request; Google technically processes your IP address. An announcement can include an in-game gift (e.g. a bomb); the gift is stored only on your device. Your phone's language (Turkish, German, English) is read only on your device to choose the game language and is not sent to any server. Basis: performance of a contract and legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(b), (f)).

Recipients and transfers. Data is processed by Google (Firebase, AdMob, Google Play) and may be transferred outside the EU (e.g. USA), secured by EU Standard Contractual Clauses and/or the EU-US Data Privacy Framework.

Retention. Ranking data is kept until you delete it (in the game or by email); monthly rankings may be kept for past months. Local data stays on your device until you uninstall.

Deletion and your rights. You can delete your ranking data in the game under Legal info → Delete my ranking data. Other local data is deleted when you uninstall. If you cannot access the app, you can also request deletion of your ranking data by emailing kodprogaming@gmail.com with your nickname (we reply within 30 days). Under the GDPR you have the right of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, objection, data portability and to withdraw consent (withdrawal does not affect earlier processing). Requests: kodprogaming@gmail.com. You may complain to the Landesbeauftragte für Datenschutz und Informationsfreiheit Nordrhein-Westfalen (LDI NRW) or the supervisory authority where you live.

Changes. This policy may be updated; the current version is published on this page.
